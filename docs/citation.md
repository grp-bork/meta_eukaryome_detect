# Citation

A paper describing the method is in preparation:

> Maistrenko OM, Kim CY, Nishijima S, Fullam A, et al. Identification of eukaryotes as a bycatch in
> global microbiome data. In preparation.

Until it is published, please cite the repository and the database:

> Fullam A. meta_eukaryome_detect: Pathogen, Parasite, Eukaryote and Virus detection in
> metagenomes. <https://github.com/grp-bork/meta_eukaryome_detect>
>
> metaEukaryome database (v1.0.0). Zenodo. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24198236>

Please also cite the tools and databases it is built on:

* **ngless**: Coelho LP, Alves R, Monteiro P, Huerta-Cepas J, Freitas AT, Bork P. NG-meta-profiler:
  fast processing of metagenomes using NGLess, a domain-specific language. *Microbiome* 7, 84 (2019).
  <https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-019-0684-8>
* **BWA**: Li H, Durbin R. Fast and accurate short read alignment with Burrows–Wheeler transform.
  *Bioinformatics* 25, 1754–1760 (2009). <https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btp324>
* **samtools**: Danecek P, Bonfield JK, Liddle J, et al. Twelve years of SAMtools and BCFtools.
  *GigaScience* 10, giab008 (2021). <https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giab008>
* **PR2**: Guillou L, Bachar D, Audic S, et al. The Protist Ribosomal Reference database (PR2): a
  catalog of unicellular eukaryote Small Sub-Unit rRNA sequences with curated taxonomy.
  *Nucleic Acids Research* 41, D597–D604 (2013). <https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gks1160>

The database was built with:

* **fastANI**: Jain C, Rodriguez-R LM, Phillippy AM, Konstantinidis KT, Aluru S. High throughput ANI
  analysis of 90K prokaryotic genomes reveals clear species boundaries. *Nature Communications* 9,
  5114 (2018). <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-07641-9>
* **CD-HIT**: Fu L, Niu B, Zhu Z, Wu S, Li W. CD-HIT: accelerated for clustering the next-generation
  sequencing data. *Bioinformatics* 28, 3150–3152 (2012). <https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/bts565>
